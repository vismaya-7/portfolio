Who am I?

Cybersecurity is my day job and my favourite puzzle. As a threat intelligence analyst, I follow attackers where they gather and report what they're planning.

Every day I track ransomware groups, spot leaked source code and confirm breaches for FalconFeeds, and I write up the findings so a security team knows what happened and what to do next.

I like working with people who take defence seriously. If you want clear threat reporting, a second pair of eyes on your security, or someone to train your team, let's talk.
